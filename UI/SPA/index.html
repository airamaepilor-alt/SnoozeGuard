02. ABOUT
face
Biometric Layer · V-01

Yawn Detection

Camera captures mouth-open events using MediaPipe Face Landmarker's 478 facial landmarks. Jaw-open blend shape coefficient ≥ 0.70 triggers a yawn event, distinguishing drowsiness-induced yawning from normal speech.

arrow_downward
Kinetic Layer · V-02

Head Drop

Nose-tip position tracked frame-by-frame across pitch and roll axes. Sudden forward drops signal a nodding-off event, triggering immediate audio feedback before loss of vehicle control occurs.

accessibility_new
Posture Layer · V-03

Head Tilt

Sustained lateral tilt beyond threshold for 10 seconds escalates directly to a Level 8 critical alert overlay. Detects gravitational posture shifts before they become dangerous steering deviations.

speed
Telemetry Layer · V-04

Sudden Brake

Accelerometer delta > 0.45 g triggers an independent brake alert. Cross-references kinetic events with facial drowsiness level for contextual escalation — identifying panic responses from microsleep.

Alert Level Scale

Drowsiness is scored 0–10. Alerts begin at Level 6 and escalate based on accumulated events. Level 9–10 activates IoT hardware and auto-SMS to emergency contacts.

Levels 1–5 — Normal, no alert triggered
Levels 6–7 — Caution, voice + IoT pulse alert
Level 8 — High alert, pull over advised
Levels 9–10 — Emergency, EC notified + IoT continuous

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using SnoozeGuard ("the App"), you agree to these Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the App. These terms apply to all users.

2. Purpose of the App

SnoozeGuard is a research and thesis project designed to detect driver drowsiness in real time using your phone's front camera and motion sensors. The App is intended to assist — not replace — safe driving practices. It does not guarantee prevention of accidents.

3. Camera and Sensor Use

The App uses your front-facing camera during active driving sessions to monitor facial landmarks (eye openness, yawning, head position). Camera frames are processed locally on your device using MediaPipe Face Landmarker ML; no video or images are transmitted to any server.

Motion sensor data (accelerometer) is used to detect sudden braking events. This data is also processed locally and only aggregated statistics are stored.

4. Location Data

Location access is requested only when a critical drowsiness alert (Level 9–10) is triggered and goes unacknowledged for 2 minutes. At that point, your current GPS coordinates are sent to your designated emergency contacts via PhilSMS. Location is not continuously tracked or stored.

5. Emergency Contact Notifications

By setting emergency contacts, you authorize the App to automatically send your name, phone number, and current GPS location to those contacts if a Level 9–10 drowsiness alert goes unacknowledged for 2 minutes. You are responsible for informing your emergency contacts that they may receive such alerts.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (cloud) and locally on your device via SQLite. Local data enables offline access to your history and emergency contact information. Supabase applies industry-standard TLS encryption in transit and at rest.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as required for the emergency notification feature (PhilSMS) or by law.
