The Science of Vigilance.

SnoozeGuard is a specialized computer vision framework developed as a Master's Thesis project to combat driver fatigue. Utilizing advanced facial landmarking and inertial telemetry, the system creates a multi-layered safety net for night-time transit.

System Core
Neural Monitoring

Detection Vectors

Our proprietary algorithm analyzes three distinct behavioral patterns to predict microsleep onset before it happens.

face

Yawning Analysis

Continuous monitoring of oral aperture dynamics. The system distinguishes between conversational speech and fatigue-induced yawning using 68-point facial landmark mapping.

Biometric Layer V-01
rebase_edit

Head Tilt

Detecting gravitational shifts in posture. Sudden forward or lateral "nodding" movements trigger immediate auditory feedback to prevent loss of control.

Kinetic Layer V-02
emergency_home

Sudden Braking

Integrated accelerometer telemetry. Analyzing deceleration patterns to identify panic responses or delayed reaction times typical of drowsy driving.

Research Philosophy

The Problem

"Fatigue accounts for 20% of all motorway accidents. Our research focuses on sub-second detection—the critical window where life-saving intervention is still possible."

The Methodology

Combining OpenCV image processing with real-time sensor fusion. The dataset includes over 1,200 hours of simulated night driving conditions and diverse lighting scenarios.
